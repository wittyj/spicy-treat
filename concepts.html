🌶 Spicy Treat

Pastry with a kick.

Three interactive 3D website concepts for Spicy Treat. Each one is a full, working website design you can click, drag, scroll and play, built on the same real menu. Open each one and try it on your phone and laptop.

You are: the baker

1 · Pepper Kitchen

Pick a stuffed roll and set the heat, from Small Pepper to Fire Dey, and watch the flames and pepper flakes grow. Then bake it, box it in a branded kraft box and send the order on WhatsApp.

You are: the office lunch champion

2 · Lagos Lunch Run

Scroll to ride an okada down a 3D Lagos street, past a landmark for every menu section. Then play "Beat the 10am Rush" and collect your office's orders before the cutoff.

You are: a spice character

3 · Heat Quest

Choose Small Pepper, Ata Dey or Fire Dey and walk around a floating bakery island. Each stall is a menu section, and your heat level picks the rolls recommended for you.
